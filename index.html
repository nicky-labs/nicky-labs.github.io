About

Growing up in Thailand made me curious about how people, cultures, and everyday environments influence the choices we make. Having lived in Thailand, France, the United States, and China, I’ve learned that the best way to understand a place is to stay long enough to experience its rhythms—not simply pass through it.

Professionally, I’ve worked across market research, consumer insights, pricing, and commercial strategy at NielsenIQ, Ipsos, and L’Oréal. I’m equally interested in analysis and execution: discovering the insight, communicating it clearly, and turning it into action.

Now, I’m combining that foundation with technology and AI to create useful research tools, digital products, and content—including Behind the Beauty Shelf.
