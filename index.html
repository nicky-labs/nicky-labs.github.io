About

Having lived in Thailand, France, the United States, and China, I’ve learned that truly understanding a place requires more than passing through—it means staying long enough to observe its rhythms, connect with its people, and appreciate the perspectives that shape everyday life. Each move has also been a journey of self-discovery, shaping how I adapt, connect, and see the world.

Professionally, I’ve worked across market research, consumer insights, pricing, and commercial strategy at NielsenIQ, Ipsos, and L’Oréal. I’m drawn to both analysis and execution: uncovering the insight, communicating it clearly, and using AI to translate it into practical action.

Today, I’m combining that foundation with technology and AI to build research tools, digital products, and content—including Behind the Beauty Shelf and Behind the Tech Stack.
