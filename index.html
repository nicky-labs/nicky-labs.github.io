About me

Market researcher, strategist, and AI builder.

Growing up in Thailand sparked my curiosity about people, cultures, and the forces that shape their choices. Having lived in Thailand, France, the United States, and China, I’ve learned to experience places deeply—staying long enough to understand how a city works rather than simply passing through it.

Professionally, I have worked across market research, consumer insights, pricing, and commercial strategy at NielsenIQ, Ipsos, and L’Oréal. I’m equally interested in analysis and execution: discovering the insight, communicating it clearly, and turning it into action.

Now, I’m combining that foundation with technology and AI to create useful research tools, digital products, and content—including Behind the Beauty Shelf.
